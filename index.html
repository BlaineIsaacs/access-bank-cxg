05 · Communication AI

More than 20 AI features come with every seat.

QContact's Communication AI (CAI) is built into the platform. These features carry no usage fees.

Every conversation

Summaries and transcription

CAI summarises every call, chat and case, and transcribes every call.

Every customer

Automatic CSAT and sentiment

CAI rates every conversation for satisfaction and sentiment, so coverage reaches 100% of contacts.

Every agent

Copilot and suggested replies

Agents get drafted replies from the knowledge base. Managers ask questions about the contact centre in plain English.

Every queue

Categorisation and routing

CAI tags the reason for contact, detects language and filters spam before a conversation reaches an agent.

Also included: CAI Biometric voice authentication for users, CAI Clarity noise removal, knowledge articles generated from documents and conversations, and CAI MCP to connect QContact to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.

06 · Agentic AI

AI agents resolve routine contacts and hand the rest to a person.

QContact's agentic AI handles voice and text conversations 24 hours a day. When a query needs a person, the AI transfers it to an agent with the full conversation attached.
